Who we are

One connected perspective on business.

Connect-Consulting Company Ltd is a consulting business based in Kigali, Rwanda. Our work brings together business development, finance consultancy and customer experience so organisations can consider their priorities with a broader view.

We believe sound advice begins with a good understanding of the organisation, its people and the environment in which it operates. We listen first, shape the work around the need, and keep attention on practical next steps.
