Who we are

A trusted B2B partner for purposeful progress.

Connect-Consulting Company Ltd is an East Africa-focused consultancy based in Kigali, Rwanda. We bring together business development, finance consultancy and customer experience to help organisations strengthen growth, financial resilience and customer relationships.

We combine strategic insight with practical execution, starting by listening to each organisation’s priorities and shaping the work around the needs of the engagement.
